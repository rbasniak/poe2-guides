Core route

Three-phase skill progression

The build is not “Ice Shot from minute one.” The run uses a faster practical progression and only becomes full Ice Shot after the Act 3 breakpoint.

Act 1 → early Act 2
Lightning

Lightning Arrow + Lightning Rod for early clear and boss setup. Use Frost Bomb as soon as possible.

Act 2 bossing
Snipe

Snipe + Frost Bomb becomes the boss-kill engine. Lightning Rod can be dropped once Snipe is online.

Act 3 → T15
Ice Shot

Ice Shot clear + Snipe bossing. The chill-magnitude quiver is the biggest damage spike.

Act 1 priorities
  • Movement speed boots if found.
  • Any usable bow with flat elemental damage.

Act 3 trap
Do not waste the Herald of Ice detour.

Take Herald of Ice if it naturally appears. If it requires backtracking, skip it. The run spends time searching and Ice Shot still clears without it.

Before the Act 3 final, confirm Snipe, Frost Bomb, and Ice Shot are all usable. The run hits level 41, upgrades Frost Bomb and Snipe, then temporarily breaks requirements. Fix attributes before entering the boss arena.
Act 4 + Interlude

Craft one strong bow, then stop over-shopping

Weapon-set passives

Use one setup for Dex/bow skills and another for Int/Frost Bomb. This prevents skill breakage when upgrading gems.

Bow benchmark

The Act 4 crafted/quality bow is good enough to carry into at least Tier 10 mapping. Do not chase tiny upgrades afterward.

Use Combat Frenzy + charge scaling if ready. Use Wind Dancer if charge requirements block the setup.

Bossing

Copy this rotation

1
Position first

Do not begin Snipe while the boss is phasing, moving, or about to become untargetable.

2
Apply Frost Bomb

Only when it is usable. If level/stat requirements block it, continue with Ice Shot/Snipe.

3
Chill/freeze and mark

Use Ice Shot, Escape Shot, or mark setup to create the damage window.

4
Fully charge Snipe

Release during the freeze/chill window. Most failed one-shots are timing failures, not build failures.

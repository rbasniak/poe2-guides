Strict order summary

Fast path through the build

  1. Act 1: Lightning Arrow + Lightning Rod. Prioritize flat attack damage, early support gems, Shortbow at lvl 5, Warden Bow at lvl 11.
  2. Act 2 start: craft a serious lvl 16 Recurve Bow. Save lvl 6/7 gems and Jeweller currency.
  3. Lvl 24: keep Lightning Arrow for clear, swap bossing to Snipe + Freezing Mark + Freezing Salvo.
  4. Lvl 31: swap Lightning Arrow to Ice Shot and Herald of Thunder to Herald of Ice; configure weapon set 2 for Freezing Mark.
  5. Lvl 33: craft ilvl 33 Dualstring/Cultist Bow. This should carry to lvl 46.
  6. Lvl 46: craft ilvl 46 Artillery Bow. This is the final campaign bow and can carry into maps.
  7. Interludes: treat them like acts: collect links, skill points, rewards, and finish cleanup before maps.
Important mismatch handled: the full VOD transcript mentions the Snipe swap around lvl 22, while the current written guide/checklist uses lvl 24. Use lvl 24 as the safer current-guide breakpoint; swap earlier only if you already have the gems and know the route.

Level 1–14 · Act 1 foundation

Use Lightning Arrow + Lightning Rod. Your priority is flat damage, early support gems, and a clean Act 1 boss order.

Gear focus

  • Flat damage to attacks on rings, gloves, quiver, and bow is the highest early value.
  • Red Vale lvl 5: farm Shortbow from Refined Arms, then Transmute/Augment bow + quiver.
  • Level 11: swap into Warden Bow; keep it cheap/magic, do not spend premium currency.
  • Movement-speed boots are very high value; do not replace MS boots unless new boots also have MS.
  • Use life/resist/evasion gear elsewhere; helmet/belt/amulet can solve attributes.

Notes

  • Act 1 is the weakest part of most bow starts. Do not under-invest in flat attack damage.
  • Pounce is optional. Use it for speed; skip it if you want fewer buttons.

Level 15–23 · Act 2 setup and Recurve Bow

Keep Lightning Arrow + Lightning Rod, but put real resources into the lvl 16 Recurve Bow and start saving gems for the Snipe swap.

Gear focus

  • Level 16 Recurve Bow is the first major investment bow.
  • Shop vendor every level for Recurve Bow bases, flat damage gloves, rings, and movement-speed boots.
  • If vendor is bad, gamble bows after the gambling vendor unlocks.
  • Best bow prefixes: high % physical damage first, then high flat physical/elemental damage.
  • When the bow is good enough: quality it, socket it, rune it, Regal/Exalt/Alch as resources allow.

Notes

  • The creator repeatedly stresses that Act 2 feels weak if the bow is bad. A good lvl 16 bow can be the difference between smooth bosses and painful two-rotation fights.
  • If lvl 16 RNG is terrible, you can delay a big craft briefly, but do not enter the Snipe phase with a truly weak bow.

Gear focus

  • Keep using the lvl 16 bow until lvl 33 unless you find an exceptional replacement.
  • Look for the league mechanic around the Act 2/late-Act-2 zone that grants the guaranteed Lesser Jeweller’s Orb/four-link opportunity.
  • Use the first Lesser Jeweller’s Orb on Snipe.
  • Quality priority starts to matter: Freezing Mark quality helps freeze buildup.

Notes

  • The full-run VTT performs this swap earlier, around lvl 22. The updated guide text points to lvl 24, so this checklist follows lvl 24 but records the earlier VOD timing.
  • Snipe timing: hold until the character flashes/lights up, then release. Practice the timing before relying on it for bosses.

Gear focus

  • Level 33 bow upgrade: ilvl 33 Dualstring Bow or Cultist Bow.
  • Cultist Bow is fine despite chaos implicit/identity because the base physical damage is strong.
  • Craft only on ilvl 33+ so the next useful damage affix tiers are available.
  • Fully invest again: 20 quality, two artificer sockets, two runes, and 6 mods if the base is good.
  • Save one Iron Rune for the lvl 46 Artillery Bow if possible.
  • Start looking for better quivers; +levels and high flat damage are still priorities.

Notes

  • Weapon swapping is not optional value here: it makes Freezing Mark last longer and makes bosses easier to freeze.
  • For general clear, Ice-Tipped Arrows + Freezing Mark + Ice Shot is usually enough.

Gear focus

  • Level 46 Artillery Bow is the last campaign bow upgrade and can carry into maps.
  • Make sure the Artillery Bow is ilvl 46 before heavy crafting.
  • Best stat is high % physical damage; if unavailable, use high flat rolls/high DPS.
  • Upgrade flasks at lvl 40 and lvl 50.
  • At lvl 41+, gamble or shop quivers if yours is weak; +2 projectile quiver is a high roll but not mandatory.

Notes

  • The VOD notes that a few bosses cannot be frozen or have mandatory phases. For those, just use normal damage rotations and do not wait forever for freeze.
  • A mediocre bow usually means two Snipe rotations; a good bow usually means one.
